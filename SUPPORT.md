# Support

- **Questions:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/crossbind/crossbind/discussions).
- **Bugs:** an [issue](https://github.com/crossbind/crossbind/issues) with the crossbind version (`crossbind --version`),
  your Node.js version and operating system, the platform you build for, the command you ran with its full output, and
  a small project or the steps that show the bug.
- **Vulnerabilities:** a private report, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says; never a public issue.
- **Your crossbind cloud account and data:** [privacy@crossbind.dev](mailto:privacy@crossbind.dev).
- **Anything else:** [support@crossbind.dev](mailto:support@crossbind.dev).

The [guide](https://crossbind.dev/guide/) and its [troubleshooting page](https://crossbind.dev/guide/troubleshooting/)
answer most questions, and [crossbind.dev/support](https://crossbind.dev/support/) says the same as this page.
